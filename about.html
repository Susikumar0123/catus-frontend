About Cerood

Cerood is a home appliance service platform that helps customers book doorstep repair, installation and maintenance services across Tamil Nadu.

Home appliance service, made simple.

Cerood is designed to make appliance servicing easier for customers. Instead of searching individually for local technicians, customers can choose the required service, submit a booking and track the service through Cerood.

We support a growing range of household appliance services including repair, installation, uninstallation, maintenance and selected appliance-specific service requirements.

Services available through Cerood

  • Smart TV and LED TV Repair
  • TV Wall Mount Installation
  • Air Conditioner Service and Repair
  • AC Installation and Uninstallation
  • Washing Machine Repair
  • Refrigerator Repair
  • RO Water Purifier Service
  • Ceiling Fan and selected home appliance services

How Cerood works

1. Easy Online Booking

Select the required service and submit your booking online.

2. Technician Assignment

A suitable service professional is assigned to the booking based on service and location availability.

3. Booking Tracking

Customers can track important booking updates through their Cerood account.

4. Service Support

Eligible repairs include post-service support under Cerood's 30-day service warranty policy.

Service coverage

Cerood is building its service network across Tamil Nadu. Service availability may vary depending on the selected appliance, location and technician availability.

Our service-area pages help customers check available appliance services across supported districts and localities.

Our goal

Our goal is to make local home appliance service easier to discover, book and manage through one simple platform while helping customers connect with suitable service professionals.

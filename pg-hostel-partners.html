Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Must we replace our current maintenance vendor?

No. Cerood can be discussed as an additional appliance service option.

Can we enquire about several washing machines or RO units together?

Yes. List the equipment and location. We will check capacity, scope and estimates before confirming any work.

Is there an automatic annual maintenance contract?

No. Any recurring plan or contract needs separate discussion and written agreement.

Are all services available in every location?

No. Equipment type, local technician availability and specialist requirements must be confirmed first.

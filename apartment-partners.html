Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to replace our current maintenance vendor?

No. Cerood is proposed as an additional resident appliance booking option.

Does the apartment association pay a monthly fee?

No monthly association fee is proposed for the trial resident-referral arrangement. Residents receive applicable service estimates before work begins.

Can we share a QR poster in our residents group?

Yes, once the association approves and Cerood confirms the area's service coverage, we can share a booking link or QR poster.

Are all services available immediately?

Availability depends on the resident's location, service category and local technician capacity. Cerood confirms before assigning work.

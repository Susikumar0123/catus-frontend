Frequently asked questions

Before you enquire

Do we need to stop using our current technician or contractor?

No. The proposed Cerood partnership can be discussed as an additional appliance service option.

Is Cerood available for every appliance in every location?

No. Available services depend on the specific location, technician capacity and the requested work. We confirm coverage before making arrangements.

Can apartments share a booking QR with residents?

Yes, after the association approves and Cerood confirms service coverage for that location.

Does this include lifts, fire systems or medical equipment?

No. These pages concern household and non-medical property appliances, not specialist safety-critical or medical equipment maintenance.

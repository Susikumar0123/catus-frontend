Chief Executive Officer

Susikumar Soundarraj founded Cerood and leads its business operations and development, bringing together Cerood Home Services and the development of Cerood’s e-commerce platform.

Leading Cerood

As founder, Susikumar oversees business direction, platform development and day-to-day operations. His work includes developing the customer experience and building relationships with service technicians and product sellers.

What Cerood is building

Cerood Home Services focuses on home repair and service needs. Cerood’s e-commerce platform is being developed to connect customers with product sellers. Each part has its own customer journey.

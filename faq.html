Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Cerood doorstep appliance repair and home services.

Booking & Service

How can I book a service with Cerood?

Choose the service you need, enter your location and complete the booking. Cerood will process your service request based on technician availability in your area.

Which home appliances does Cerood service?

Cerood provides services for appliances such as Smart TVs, air conditioners, washing machines, refrigerators and RO water purifiers.

Does Cerood provide doorstep service?

Yes. Available services are provided at the customer's location, subject to technician availability in the selected service area.

Service Areas

Where is Cerood service available?

Cerood service availability depends on the selected district, city, town or local service area. You can check available services through the Service Areas section.

What if service is not available in my location?

Service availability may vary by location and technician availability. You can check Cerood again as coverage expands.

Pricing & Repairs

Is the displayed price the final repair cost?

The final cost may depend on the appliance condition, required repair work and spare parts. Any additional repair requirements can be confirmed after inspection.

Are spare parts included in the service price?

Spare-part charges may be separate depending on the repair required. Applicable charges should be confirmed before the additional work is carried out.

Technicians

How is a technician assigned to my booking?

Cerood assigns service requests based on technician availability and service coverage for the selected location.

Can technician availability vary by location?

Yes. Technician availability can vary depending on the service, location and requested booking time.
